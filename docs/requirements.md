## Design Questions

### 1. What should Finn physically look like?
Answer: Finn will have a 3d printed outer shell, with the interior containing all hardware for him to work. The 3d printed shell is undetermined but I know that it will be small enough to not be a desk distraction but big enough to contatin hardware.

### 2. How should Finn interact with the user?
Answer: Finn will interact through intentional user input. In response, Finn will use facial expressions, lights, robotic sounds, and simple physical movement. Finn will not independently listen to or monitor the user.

### 3. What should his face be capable of?
Answer: His face will be capable of portraying emotion through its "eyes." 

### 4. What should physically move?
Answer: Finn will have limited physical movement used for expression rather than mobility. Finn's head or face assembly will be able to rotate from side to side. Finn will not be able to drive, walk, or navigate independently.

### 5. What should Finn sound like?
Answer: Finn will sound like a robot, nothing will portray human tendencies except emotion and its "face".

### 6. Should Finn be able to hear?
Answer: This version of Finn will not listen to anything. It's interactions will be user intended.

### 7. Should Finn connect to the internet?
Answer: Finn v1.0 will not require an internet connection. Its primary interactions and behaviors will operate locally.

### 8. How should he be powered?
Answer: Rechargable batteries.

### 9. What gives Finn personality?
Answer: Finn's personality will be expressed through facial expressions, lights, robotic sounds, and simple physical movement. Different combinations of these outputs will allow Finn to portray different emotions and reactions.

### 10. What is the defining feature of Finn?
Answer: Finn is a small robotic companion that responds to intentional user interactions with expressive, personality-driven responses through its face, lights, sounds, and movement.
