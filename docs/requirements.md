## Design Questions

### 1. What should Finn physically look like?
Answer: Finn will have a 3d printed outer shell, with the interior containing all hardware for him to work. The 3d printed shell is undetermined but I know that it will be small enough to not be a desk distraction but big enough to contatin hardware.

### 2. How should Finn interact with the user?
Answer: Finn will be able to interact simply. Finn will respond with basic face movement, lights, and sound. It will be a limited set of simple interactions.

### 3. What should his face be capable of?
Answer: His face will be capable of portraying emotion through its "eyes." 

### 4. What should physically move?
Answer: I think for now the face screen will "move" but nothing physically will actually move.

### 5. What should Finn sound like?
Answer: Finn will sound like a robot, nothing will portray human tendencies except emotion and its "face".

### 6. Should Finn be able to hear?
Answer: This version of Finn will not listen to anything. It's interactions will be user intended.

### 7. Should Finn connect to the internet?
Answer: N/A

### 8. How should he be powered?
Answer: Rechargable batteries.

### 9. What gives Finn personality?
Answer: Face, lights, and sound

### 10. What is the defining feature of Finn?
Answer: Finn is a small companion that responds to intentional user interactions with expressive, personality driven response.
